Evolution of Computer Vision

1998
LeNet-5
First successful CNN for digit recognition. 62K parameters.
2012
AlexNet
Won ImageNet, started the deep learning revolution. ReLU, Dropout, GPU training.
2014
VGGNet & GoogLeNet
Deeper networks with 3×3 convs. Inception modules.
2015
ResNet & U-Net
Skip connections enable 152+ layer networks. Encoder-decoder for segmentation.
2017
DenseNet & Transformers
Dense connections. Attention Is All You Need paper.
2019
EfficientNet
Compound scaling for optimal efficiency. NAS-designed architecture.
2020
ViT & DDPM
Transformers for vision. Denoising diffusion probabilistic models.
2021
CLIP & Swin Transformer
Vision-language pretraining. Hierarchical vision transformer.
2022
Stable Diffusion
Open-source text-to-image generation. Latent diffusion.
2023
SAM & GPT-4V
Segment Anything Model. Vision-language models.
